Our Journey

1991

Foundation in Tanzania

The Little Sisters of St. Francis began their mission in Tanzania with the establishment of St. Clare Convent in Chanjale Seminary-Mwanga District, part of the Kilimanjaro Region in the Diocese of Same.

2010

Decision for Independence

The General Chapter decided to elevate Tanzania to a separate Region due to geographical distance from the East of the Rift Valley Region, which encompassed various Dioceses in Kenya and Tanzania.

December 14, 2013

Official Inauguration

The North-Eastern Region Tanzania was officially inaugurated, initially comprising Arusha Archdiocese and Same Diocese in the Kilimanjaro Region, with Sr. Regina Namagheri as the first Regional Superior.

2017

Expansion of Service

The North-Eastern Region Tanzania extended its services to include Shinyanga Diocese and Morogoro Diocese, expanding our reach and impact across Tanzania.

Present

Continued Growth

Today, we are served by 67 Sisters across 12 Communities, engaged in various apostolates including education, medical services, communication, secretarial work, school matrons, social work, and development management.
